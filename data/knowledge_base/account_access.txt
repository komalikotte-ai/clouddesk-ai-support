CLOUDDESK ACCOUNT ACCESS FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: On the CloudDesk login page, click Forgot Password. Enter your registered email address and follow the password reset link sent to your email.

Q: My account is locked. What should I do?
A: Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts. Wait 15 minutes and try again. If the account remains locked, contact Account Support.

Q: How do I change my email address?
A: Open Settings > Profile and select Change Email. Enter the new email address and complete the verification process.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Open Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup instructions.

Q: I did not receive my password reset email.
A: Check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the registered email address. If the email still does not arrive, contact Account Support.
